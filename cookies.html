cookies & digital crumbs

Like the mycelial networks beneath a forest floor, cookies help us understand how you move through the alcove — anonymously, respectfully, and always with your privacy in mind.

what are cookies?

Cookies are small text files that are stored on your device when you visit a website. They help us remember your preferences, understand how you interact with our content, and improve your experience. We use cookies sparingly and never for intrusive tracking.

how we use cookies

At Quelyra Sunthorn Alcove, we use cookies for three purposes:

  • Essential: to keep the site functional (e.g., remembering your cookie preferences).
  • Analytics: to count visits and see which articles resonate most — always aggregated and anonymised.
  • Preference: to remember your display choices (like dark mode, if we ever add it).

We do not use advertising cookies, nor do we share your data with third-party platforms. Your visit remains a quiet one.

managing cookies

You can control and delete cookies through your browser settings. Most browsers allow you to block third‑party cookies, clear existing ones, or opt out of analytics. For more detailed instructions, visit the help section of your browser.

We recommend keeping essential cookies enabled to ensure the alcove works as intended, but the choice is entirely yours.

changes to this policy

We may update this Cookies Policy from time to time to reflect changes in technology or regulation. Any updates will be posted here, and we will notify you via the website if they are significant.
